How do I get these values?

See README.md for the full walkthrough. In short: convert your IG account to Business/Creator and link it to a Facebook Page, create a Meta app, grant instagram_basic, generate a long-lived token, and get your IG user ID. The Discord webhook URL comes from Channel Settings → Integrations → Webhooks → New Webhook → Copy URL.
